# My Favorite Movie

## The Hangover (2009)

**The Hangover** is my favorite comedy movie because it is hilarious, entertaining, and full of unexpected situations. The story follows four friends who travel to Las Vegas for a bachelor party, but things get complicated when they wake up with no memory of what happened the night before.

### Why I Like It
- Very funny and entertaining scenes
- Unexpected plot twists
- Great chemistry between the characters
- Unique and memorable storyline

### Movie Information
- **Genre:** Comedy
- **Release Year:** 2009
- **Director:** Todd Phillips
